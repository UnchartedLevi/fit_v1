FITS STOREFRONT-ONLY LAUNCH MODE
================================

Status: ENABLED

The primary domain (fits4l.xyz) and the shop subdomain (shop.fits4l.xyz)
currently present the same shop-first experience.

While this temporary launch mode is enabled:

- Visiting the root URL (/) is internally rewritten to the products page.
- The original marketing homepage is hidden, not deleted. Its implementation
  remains in src/app/page.tsx for later use.
- The Shop, Spotlight, and Our Journey navigation links are hidden on both
  domains. The storefront search, account, and bag controls remain visible.
- Existing pages such as /about and /spotlight remain in the codebase and can
  still be reached directly, but they are not linked from the public header.

The shared switch is STOREFRONT_ONLY_MODE in:

  src/lib/storefront-mode.ts

To restore the separate marketing homepage and normal primary-domain header,
set STOREFRONT_ONLY_MODE to false. The shop subdomain will continue to use the
shop experience through its existing hostname detection.

Do not delete the hidden homepage or navigation implementation unless the
project owner explicitly approves replacing it permanently.
